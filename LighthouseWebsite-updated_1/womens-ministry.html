Women's Ministry

Who We Are

Built for
Every Woman

Women's Ministry at Lighthouse Church is a place where women of all ages and walks of life can encounter God, find genuine community, and grow in their faith. Whether you're new to faith or have walked with Jesus for years, you belong here.

Stay connected with us on Instagram for the latest announcements, events, and community updates.

✦

Community

A safe, welcoming space where real friendships are formed and every woman is known and valued.

✦

✦

Events
